This illustration allows both views. Actual deductibility and tax treatment depend on CRA rules, investment type, and the client's personal tax situation.

合资格的投资借款利息可能在其支付或应付的年度抵减应税收入（须符合 CRA 规则）。利息成本并不会消失，但税务抵扣可能降低借款的税后实际成本。 Eligible investment loan interest may reduce taxable income in the year it is paid or payable, subject to CRA rules. The interest cost does not disappear, but the tax deduction may reduce the real after-tax cost of borrowing.
